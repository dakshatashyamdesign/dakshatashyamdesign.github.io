Reel 01 — Project brief

The brief

Design a chewing gum pack that holds 10 pieces, inspired by Retro Maximalism and the nostalgia of classic cinema.

The pack had to carry the charm of old movie posters (vintage type, bold colour and layered graphics) and turn it into something people enjoy looking at and remember after it leaves their hands.

Meet Chumma Chew

சும்மா (chumma) is Tamil for “just like that”. Chumma Chew is a gum brand for digital-native youth who chew, scroll and multitask all at once.

The pack speaks in desi maximalism (loud colours, busy type and meme-style slang) and hides one smart trick: its secondary slab folds into a simple phone stand that sits on a desk or study table.

Reel 02 — Research

Know the chew

A

What a gum pack must do

  • Portable
  • Protects
  • Keeps it fresh
  • Easy dispensing
  • Food safe

It has to be small enough to carry every day while keeping out moisture and contamination. It also has to show the essentials (ingredients, manufacturing details and branding) clearly, without spoiling how it looks.

B

The FMCG reality

Gum is a Fast-Moving Consumer Good: inexpensive, bought often and used daily. Products like this win on strong branding and shelf visibility. The catch:

  • Tiny pack size
  • Little room for info & graphics
  • Production cost limits
  • Material durability
  • Function vs. maximalism

    Reel 05 — Learnings

    After the credits

    01

    Give the pack a second life

    Building the phone stand into the dieline taught me to design for what happens after the product is used up. Utility is what turns a cheap wrapper into something people keep.

    02

    Maximalism still needs order

    Loud borders and busy type only work when the hierarchy underneath is strict. The flavour, the 2-in-1 callout and the info panel each needed their own clear zone.

    03

    Prove it in paper

    Folding rough models showed which forms could actually dispense gum and hold a phone upright, and pinned down measurements that sketches alone couldn’t.

    04

    Culture is the hook

    Tamil lettering, cinema trivia and temple-poster colour gave a small FMCG pack a personality the audience recognises and wants to share.
