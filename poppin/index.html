01 Project brief

Hot popcorn. Anywhere. In seconds.

Popp'in is a food-tech brand built around a self-warming, single-serve popcorn pod. It puts hot popcorn in people's hands wherever they are, so no one has to settle for a cold snack or go looking for an appliance. The brief: build the brand from scratch, from strategy and identity to packaging and an India-wide launch.

02 Where the idea came from

Born on a six-hour train ride

Hot popcorn is one of the world's most loved snacks, yet for 35 years getting it hot has depended on a microwave or oven, fixed at home.

On a long train journey, the canteen was too far and the station popcorn arrived stale and cold. Self-heating hand warmers and military rations proved the technology already existed, and research showed the ingredients, supply chains and demand were ready. No one had put them together.

“Why is there no hot popcorn, right now?”

Mission

Close the gap between craving and having

Bring freshly made hot popcorn to every moment and place that has none.

Vision

Make warmth portable

A hot, satisfying snack for anyone, anywhere, with no appliance, electricity or compromise.

03 Technicalities

How a pod warms itself

01

The heat

An OEM heating patch of iron powder, salt, water, activated carbon and vermiculite oxidises to make heat with no flame or power. Temperature and duration are customisable.

02

The chamber

A PET/Al/PE laminate chamber and foil layer keep the popcorn apart from the heat pack, inside a double-wall kraft sleeve.

03

The seal

Popped at the factory, flash-cooled and nitrogen-sealed in a humidity-controlled room, so it stays fresh until it's warmed.

The kernel

Yellow mushroom, pearl type

Argentine yellow mushroom popcorn (Zea mays everta) pops into large, round balls that hold coatings well and fill the pod. It needs at least 400°F (205°C) to open fully.

Launch campaign

4-week mystery-to-reveal · India

For two weeks, no product. Just questions and heat, placed where the audience already spends its day: platforms, metros, college walls, flyovers and airports.

Wk 1–2

Seed the mystery

Blue-and-yellow posters appear overnight across seven cities, on metro pillars, station concourses, campus boards and inside trains.

Specific enough to feel personal, vague enough to make people wonder.

Wk 3

Show the pod

One product photo drops on social and updated posters. 50–80 micro-influencers get a pod and a handwritten note, and their unscripted reactions become the first content.

90 seconds. Trust it.The only words on the note sent with each influencer pod.
Wk 4

Launch day

Live activations at five transit hubs, and every mystery poster is replaced with the final campaign.

Why it works

The audience already thinks “why is there nothing warm here?” Popp'in asks it on a wall first, then answers it in the exact place the problem lives: on the platform, in their hands, just before they board.

06 Learnings

What this project taught me

  1. Research makes a brand believable

    Kernel science and real costs shaped the design, from the origami extension to the ₹99 price point.

  2. Sell the feeling, not the feature

    Self-heating is the technology, but comfort is what people buy. The tagline, voice and Poppy all carry that warmth.

  3. Timing is a design tool

    Designing for platforms and metros, and holding the product back for two weeks, made the reveal land harder.
